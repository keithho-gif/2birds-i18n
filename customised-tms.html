Moment 01 · Enquiry and quote

Quote the nett fee before the trainee commits.

A trainee's fee depends on age, citizenship and whether an employer is sponsoring the place. Nest applies the current SWDA funding rules to the course and returns the exact nett fee in the enquiry, so the quote holds when the invoice is issued.

Staff read the funding tables, work out the percentages manually and correct the quote after the enrolment is rejected.

Moment 02 · Registration

Take registration once, with every field the regulator needs.

The training provider's own registration form collects the trainee, the employer and the sponsorship in one pass. Nest checks each field against what TPGateway will ask for and assigns the registration to the consultant who brought it in.

Details arrive by email, chat and spreadsheet, and each one is typed again into the portal.

Enrolments are keyed into the portal one by one, and a rejected record is found days later.

Moment 04 · Before class

Brief trainees and trainers without writing a single email.

Joining instructions, venue directions and reminders go out by email on a schedule the training provider sets, with the right attachments and copies to employers. When a trainee needs a personal word, the administrator sends a WhatsApp message straight from the class record.

Someone sends reminders manually the night before and hopes the list is current.

Moment 05 · On the day

Mark attendance by session and act on absence at once.

Nest shows the venue, the class list and the rain forecast for the morning. When a trainee has not signed in, the administrator sends a WhatsApp message from the class screen to ask whether they are on the way or would like to postpone, and the reply is recorded against the class. Attendance goes to TPGateway from the same screen.

Paper sheets are collected, checked and uploaded after the class, when an absence can no longer be recovered.

Certificates are downloaded, renamed and emailed one at a time.

Moment 08 · Audit

Keep the evidence ready before the auditor asks.

Evidence is gathered from folders and inboxes in the weeks before the audit.

Customised to your practice

Built around the way you operate

Nest functions entirely as your proprietary training platform. Trainees and company-sponsored applicants interact exclusively with your visual identity and editorial tone, backed by enrolment workflows and administrative controls configured to your exact operating guidelines.

  • Registration form fields and consent wording reflect your organisation's internal policies and data protection standards.
  • Message templates, attachments and schedules follow the training provider's calendar.
  • Each consultant's registrations are tracked from the form to the enrolment.
  • Reports and exports match the training provider's accounts.
  • All course registrations route directly through your organisation's authorised TPGateway integration and SWDA credentials.

Without a TMS, and with Nest

Learner CorrespondenceJoining instructions, reminders, and timetable details are compiled and dispatched individually for each cohort.Pre-course briefings and required materials dispatch automatically based on intake dates and established operational templates.

Security

Built to be tested

2BIRDS is in discussions with the School of Computing at the National University of Singapore (NUS) to submit the Nest Training Management System for IFS4103. The objective is to designate Nest as an Enterprise Security Target, subjecting our codebase and architecture to rigorous evaluation by cybersecurity researchers trained to find subtle vulnerabilities.
