TPQA Audit Matters

Prepare for TPQA with established operational systems.

SWDA evaluates registered providers through the Training Provider Quality Assessment (TPQA) to monitor continuing compliance. Our team explains each regulatory standard, outlines agency governance expectations, and helps keep your routine training operations resilient and compliant.

I · The assessment

Scope of the TPQA evaluation

SWDA requires each training provider to document its governance systems and provide verifiable audit evidence of operational compliance. Select a criterion below to view its specific assessment items.

1.1Learner communication, feedback, requests, and appeals

Requirement

Learners receive accurate course information, while every enquiry, piece of feedback, and appeal is resolved and formally closed.

What assessors may look for

Information checked and approved before release, and tracked response times across all communication channels.

1.2Pre-course and post-course advice

Requirement

Prospective learners receive clear advice before enrolment, and graduates receive guidance on progression pathways after completion.

What assessors may look for

Course advisors who are formally trained and monitored, supported by documented advisory records.

1.3Administration systems

Requirement

Assessment outcomes upload accurately to TPGateway, and the digital learning platform satisfies SWDA delivery standards.

What assessors may look for

Documented operational workflows, including clear delivery procedures for planned e-learning programmes.

1.4Corporate governance

Requirement

Leadership maintains strategic plans with measurable targets, ensures sound financial oversight, and reviews internal systems regularly.

What assessors may look for

Formal management review records showing operational findings, designated owners, and agreed timelines for every follow-up action.

1.5Management of marketing activities

Requirement

All promotional activities comply with SWDA marketing standards and conditions, reinforced by a code of conduct for marketing personnel and representatives, updated from 1 December 2025.

What assessors may look for

Valid course reference numbers displayed in advertisements, and testimonials stating the learner full name, completed course, and completion year.

1.6Tracking organisational outcomes

Requirement

The outcomes established under Criterion 3 are measured consistently and used to guide organisational improvements.

What assessors may look for

Every outcome clearly defined, tracked systematically, and reviewed within scheduled management meetings.
2.1Course quality assurance

Requirement

Lesson plans, training materials, and assessment plans align fully with the approved course proposal and learning outcomes, as well as statutory skill proficiency descriptions for WSQ courses.

What assessors may look for

Documented consistency across all instructional materials, alongside recorded evidence of industry consultation.

2.2Screening and profiling learners

Requirement

Enrolment is restricted strictly to learners who satisfy all published course entry prerequisites.

What assessors may look for

Verified entry qualification certificates on file prior to course commencement, including for candidates sponsored by corporate clients.

2.3Adult educator management

Requirement

Adult educators are recruited, inducted, observed, appraised, and supported through a structured development system.

What assessors may look for

A formal code of conduct for adult educators, explicit definitions of underperformance, and documented intervention logs.

2.4Plan-Do-Check-Act

Requirement

Courses undergo scheduled evaluations according to a documented cycle using performance data and stakeholder feedback.

What assessors may look for

Completed review cycles adhering to documented schedules, incorporating learner and industry input, with assigned owners and timelines for each corrective action.
3.1Organisational outcomes

Requirement

Reported formally through the prescribed Required Information Template issued by SWDA.

What assessors may look for

Accurate and consistent metrics that reconcile fully with the internal operating records of the provider.

3.2Training outcomes

Requirement

Evaluated through the SWDA Training Quality and Outcomes Measurement (TRAQOM) framework.

What assessors may look for

Performance indicators tracked across cohorts and actively applied to enhance instructional delivery.
How the assessment runs
  1. 01

    Notification

    About three months before the site assessment, SWDA emails you four documents to prepare.

  2. 02

    Self-assessment

    Your team conducts an internal review against the SWDA self-assessment checklist.

  3. 03

    Submission

    About two months later, your Management Representative submits the documents on TPGateway. There is no fee.

  4. 04

    Onsite assessment

    SWDA assessors spend one day on site, interviewing your team and clarifying on the documents that you have submitted.

  5. 05

    Outcome

    You receive your grade and the Assessment Report on TPGateway.

The four documents are the TPQA Requirements, the Document Submission List, the Required Information Template and the Course Information Template.

EDUTRUST PROVISIONSTraining providers holding a four-year EduTrust certification are evaluated under a reduced assessment scope.

GROUNDS FOR APPEALA formal review may be requested solely if the assessment team failed to observe established procedures, or if audit findings depart manifestly from the evidence presented. An unfavourable evaluation, subsequent corrections, or supplementary documentation do not constitute valid grounds for review.

Practices that strengthen an application

Trainer observation

Managers observe a new trainer's first training session in full and deliver structured feedback at the end of the day. Managers continue routine observations periodically using a standard rubric focused on professional growth.

Response to feedback

Learner ratings are evaluated after every run. Any score falling below four out of five triggers a targeted improvement plan, with disputed findings escalated to an objective review panel.

Assessment supervision

An administrative assistant supports class operations and invigilates assessments independently, leaving the instructor to concentrate on delivery.

Pre-enrolment screening

Entry criteria are confirmed against actual certificates prior to course start dates for all enrolled learners.

Courseware consistency

Lesson schedules, learner guides, and assessment instruments align completely with the authorised curriculum proposal.

Advertising standards

Marketing materials include valid course reference numbers, and testimonials state the graduate name, course relation, and completion year.

Enquiry and appeal channels

Distinct workflows manage learner feedback, service requests, and academic appeals, with logged response standards maintained across all communication channels.

Pre-release approval

Course literature is reviewed and authorised for factual accuracy prior to learner dissemination.

Management oversight

Management reviews track key indicators and establish firm accountability by assigning an owner and resolution timeline to every action item.

Educator capability building

Adult educators participate in peer observations, full-time employees complete annual external training, and all capability development under the Training and Adult Education Professional Pathway is recorded.
