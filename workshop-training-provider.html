Course title

Establishing and Operating a Registered Training Provider

A guide to TPGateway, funding administration and the SWDA Terms

Overview Learning outcomes Programme Who should attend

Overview

Everything a new or expanding training provider requires to deliver funded courses with complete regulatory confidence, from submitting initial course applications on TPGateway to administering trainee enrolments, attendance records, and funding claims.

Most organisations learn these compliance requirements through costly trial and error, absorbing rejected course submissions and lapsed funding claims along the way. This programme replaces that friction with two days of structured operational practice.

Day one covers academic operations, enrolment workflows, courseware development, and the mechanics of funding from the Skills and Workforce Development Agency (SWDA). Day two focuses on compliant sales and marketing practices, followed by statutory legal obligations and audit readiness. Every module is taught directly against live system screens, regulatory circulars, and contractual clauses, supported by worked case examples throughout.

Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, you will be able to

  1. 01

    Execute complete TPGateway operational workflows, including course submissions, bulk trainee enrolments, attendance verification, and grant disbursements.

  2. 02

    Audit internal documentation standards to ensure assessment records, learner's documents, and statutory submissions satisfy SWDA regulatory deadlines.

  3. 03

    Determine the submission pathway, funding tier eligibility, and endorsement requirements for new course approvals.

  4. 04

    Evaluate marketing materials, third-party distributor agreements, and commercial fee practices against the SWDA Terms and Code of Practice.
