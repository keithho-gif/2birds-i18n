1.Application of these Terms

1.1

These Terms of Engagement (the "Terms") set out the conditions on which 2BIRDS PRIVATE LIMITED (UEN 202625717Z), a company incorporated in the Republic of Singapore with its registered office at 7 Temasek Boulevard, #12-07 Suntec Tower One, Singapore 038987 ("2BIRDS", "we" or "us"), provides services to its clients (each, the "Client").

1.2

These Terms apply to every engagement and are incorporated by reference into every proposal, quotation, statement of work, engagement letter and invoice issued by 2BIRDS (each an "Engagement Letter"), whether or not a copy is attached. The Engagement Letter and these Terms together form the agreement between 2BIRDS and the Client (the "Agreement"). If they conflict, these Terms prevail unless the Engagement Letter expressly states otherwise.

1.2A

Amendments to these Terms.

  1. (a)2BIRDS may amend these Terms from time to time, including the timelines for delivery, the terms of payment, the treatment of Goods and Services Tax and the rate of interest on overdue amounts, by publishing a revised version on this website.
  2. (b)For any engagement agreed on or after the date of publication, the revised version applies from that date.
  3. (c)For an engagement already in force, an amendment takes effect thirty (30) days after 2BIRDS gives the Client written notice of it by email (the "Notice Period"), and applies only to work performed and invoices issued after the Notice Period ends. No amendment affects any fee already agreed, any invoice already issued or any amount already due.
  4. (d)A Client who does not accept an amendment to an engagement already in force may end that engagement by written notice given within the Notice Period, without any termination fee or other penalty. The Client pays for work performed and costs committed up to the date the engagement ends, and the version of these Terms in force before the amendment continues to apply to that work.
  5. (e)A Client who continues to instruct 2BIRDS, or to accept or use any Deliverable, after the Notice Period ends accepts the amendment.
  6. (f)A fixed fee, fixed price or fixed date expressly agreed in an Engagement Letter may be changed only by a written variation signed by both parties, and not by an amendment under this clause.
  7. (g)Where an amendment reflects a change in law or in the requirements of the Skills and Workforce Development Agency (SWDA) or any other public authority, including any change in the rate or application of Goods and Services Tax or the registration of 2BIRDS for Goods and Services Tax, it takes effect on the date that change takes effect, and paragraphs (c) and (d) do not apply to it.
  8. (h)The version applicable to an invoice is the version in force under this clause on the date that invoice is issued.
1.3

The Client accepts these Terms, and is bound by them, on the earliest of:

  1. (a)countersigning an Engagement Letter;
  2. (b)issuing a purchase order or written instruction to proceed;
  3. (c)paying any amount invoiced by 2BIRDS; or
  4. (d)accepting or using any Deliverable.
1.4

The version of these Terms that applies to an engagement is the version published on the 2BIRDS website on the date of the Engagement Letter.

2.Definitions and interpretation

2.1

In these Terms:

  1. (a)"Competent Authority" means the Skills and Workforce Development Agency ("SWDA"), Workforce Singapore, and any other governmental, statutory or regulatory body with authority over the matters to which the Services relate;
  2. (b)"Deliverables" means the courseware, submissions, reports, documents, designs and other materials that 2BIRDS provides to the Client under the Agreement;
  3. (c)"Fees" means the professional fees for the Services set out in the Engagement Letter;
  4. (d)"Services" means the services described in the Engagement Letter, which may include curriculum and courseware development, submission support, training provider setup and operations advisory, audit preparation, practitioner sourcing and creative marketing; and
  5. (e)"Background Materials" means the methodologies, templates, frameworks, know-how and materials owned or developed by 2BIRDS independently of the engagement.
2.2

Headings are for convenience only. The words "include" and "including" are to be read without limitation. A reference to a statute includes subsidiary legislation made under it and any amendment or re-enactment of it.

3.3

Dates given in the Engagement Letter or in correspondence are good-faith estimates. They are not contractual deadlines unless the Engagement Letter expressly states that time is of the essence.

2BIRDS may perform the Services through its directors, employees and engaged practitioners, and is responsible for the Services they perform. 2BIRDS does not assume any duty beyond those expressly set out in the Agreement, and no fiduciary duty is owed.

5.Regulatory outcomes

5.1

The grant, renewal or maintenance of any registration, approval, funding, accreditation or quality grade rests solely with the relevant Competent Authority and is outside the control of 2BIRDS.

5.2

Accordingly, 2BIRDS does not warrant or guarantee that the Services or any Deliverable will result in:

  1. (a)the approval of any course, or its listing on any course directory;
  2. (b)the grant of funding at any particular rate, or at all;
  3. (c)the Client’s registration or continued registration as an Approved Training Organisation;
  4. (d)any particular outcome in a Training Provider Quality Assessment or Course Quality Check; or
  5. (e)the absence of any query, finding or request for further information from a Competent Authority.
5.3

Where a Competent Authority raises queries on a submission prepared by 2BIRDS, 2BIRDS will assist in responding to them to the extent set out in the Engagement Letter. Further work outside that scope will be agreed in writing and charged separately.

5.4

Regulatory requirements change from time to time. A Deliverable reflects the requirements in force on the date it is issued, and 2BIRDS is not obliged to update it for later changes unless separately engaged to do so.

6.Fees and expenses

6.1

The Client will pay the Fees set out in the Engagement Letter. Unless the Engagement Letter states otherwise, the Fees exclude out-of-pocket expenses, travel, third-party charges and government fees, which will be charged to the Client at cost.

6.2

2BIRDS is not registered for Goods and Services Tax. No Goods and Services Tax is charged on the Fees. If 2BIRDS becomes so registered, Goods and Services Tax will be added to the Fees at the prevailing rate.

6.3

Unless the Engagement Letter provides otherwise, the Fees for a staged engagement are payable as follows:

  1. (a)fifty per cent on countersignature of the Engagement Letter, as a commencement fee; and
  2. (b)the balance of fifty per cent on the issue of the principal Deliverable for the Client’s review, whether or not the Client then lodges it with a Competent Authority.
6.4

The Client acknowledges that the Fees have been set on the basis of the allocation of risk in these Terms, and that they would be higher if 2BIRDS were to accept greater liability or to guarantee any regulatory outcome.

7.Invoicing and payment

7.1

Invoices are payable in full, in Singapore dollars and in cleared funds, within fourteen days of the invoice date, without set-off, counterclaim or deduction except as required by law.

7.2

If the Client disputes any part of an invoice, it must notify 2BIRDS in writing within seven days of the invoice date, identifying the disputed item and the reasons. The undisputed balance remains payable on the due date. An invoice that is not disputed within that period is treated as accepted, save for manifest error.

7.3

Overdue amounts bear interest at the rate of one and a half per cent per month, calculated daily from the due date until payment in full. The Client will also reimburse the reasonable costs, including legal costs, incurred by 2BIRDS in recovering overdue amounts.

7.4

2BIRDS may suspend the Services, and withhold any Deliverable, while any amount remains overdue. 2BIRDS is not liable for any consequence of a suspension under this clause.

8.Non-refundable fees

8.1

Fees are earned on receipt and are not refundable, in whole or in part, except where a refund is required by law or expressly provided for in the Engagement Letter.

8.2

Without limiting clause 8.1, no refund is payable because:

  1. (a)a Competent Authority refuses, defers, suspends or withdraws any application, approval or registration;
  2. (b)the Client decides not to proceed with, or to withdraw, any submission;
  3. (c)the Client changes its business plans, ownership or management;
  4. (d)regulatory requirements change after a Deliverable is issued;
  5. (e)the Client does not implement or resource a Deliverable; or
  6. (f)the Client terminates the engagement for convenience.

  1. (a)lodge them with a Competent Authority in support of the Client’s own application;
  2. (b)deliver, from the Client’s approved premises and in the form approved by the Competent Authority, the course to which they relate; and
  3. (c)keep records and respond to audits and queries from a Competent Authority.

On termination of the licence, the Client will stop using the Deliverables and, if requested, return or destroy all copies and confirm in writing that it has done so.
