Course title

Workflow Automation for Training Operations

Enrolment, attendance, grant administration and reporting

Overview Learning outcomes Programme Who should attend

Overview

Training operations depend on high volumes of repetitive administration, from registration and attendance to grant claims and learner communications. This workshop shows how to automate that work while preserving the accuracy and audit trail the Skills and Workforce Development Agency (SWDA) expects.

Manual processes consume staff capacity and introduce errors that surface later as rejected claims, missed deadlines, or audit findings. Automation resolves these problems only when it is designed around the regulatory requirements it serves.

The session moves from mapping existing workflows and prioritising automation opportunities to building automations across enrolment, assessment, finance, and reporting, and concludes with the controls that keep them reliable. Every module is taught against real operational processes and statutory deadlines.

Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, you will be able to

  1. 01

    Map existing operational workflows and quantify where staff time and error risk are concentrated.

  2. 02

    Prioritise automation opportunities according to effort, risk, and regulatory impact.

  3. 03

    Design automated processes for enrolment, attendance, assessment records, and grant administration.

  4. 04

    Establish monitoring, exception handling, and audit controls that keep automated workflows accurate and compliant.

Programme

What you will learn

Part 1 · 2 modules

Mapping the work

1.1Where the time goes
  1. (a)The operational lifecycle of a course run
  2. (b)Repeated data entry and manual hand-offs
  3. (c)The deadlines that drive the workload
1.2Choosing what to automate
  1. (a)Mapping a workflow step by step
  2. (b)Rules-based tasks and tasks that need judgement
  3. (c)Estimating time saved and risk reduced

Part 2 · 3 modules

Building the automations

2.1Registration and enrolment
  1. (a)Structured registration forms
  2. (b)Automated confirmation emails to learners and trainers
  3. (c)Preparing records for bulk upload to TPGateway
2.2Attendance, assessment and follow-up
  1. (a)Reminders ahead of attendance and assessment deadlines
  2. (b)Tracking the seventy-five (75) per cent attendance threshold
  3. (c)Post-course surveys and certificate follow-up
2.3Finance and reporting
  1. (a)Tracking fee collection against the sixty (60) day deadline
  2. (b)Reconciling grants with the Monthly Grant Report
  3. (c)Dashboards for management review

Part 3 · 2 modules

Control and compliance

3.1Keeping automations accurate
  1. (a)Testing before go-live
  2. (b)Exception handling and manual review
  3. (c)Documenting each workflow
3.2Data and audit
  1. (a)Personal data under the PDPA
  2. (b)Access controls and record retention
  3. (c)Evidence that automated processes were followed

Who should attend

  • Operations and administration teams
  • Training coordinators and course managers
  • Finance staff who track grants and fee collection
  • Directors looking to scale without adding headcount
