§ III · The pairing

Paired to your project, at no cost to you, and none to the learner.

The toughest part of most curriculum development is the subject-matter expert. Without one, the Course Proposal Form cannot be submitted; with the wrong one, the courseware will not survive the Course Quality Check. We keep a working community, prepared and ready, of practitioners who share our understanding that good training ought not to be priced out of reach.

Where your project can be matched from our community, the pairing is free. We do not charge a finder's fee, a placement margin or a per-hour markup on top of the SME's own rate. Where our practitioners cannot match the project, we source through the usual recruitment channels (Jobstreet and the like) and recover only the direct costs of the search and administration.

A short note on what we are not. 2birds is not a recruitment agency. We do not hold a recruitment licence and we do not place candidates into employment. We make introductions; we do not negotiate contracts on anyone's behalf.
