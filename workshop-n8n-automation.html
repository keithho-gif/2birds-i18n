Course title

Orchestrating Intelligent Workflows with n8n

Workflow triggers, system integrations and AI agent nodes

Overview Learning outcomes Programme Who should attend

Overview

n8n has become one of the most widely adopted platforms for connecting applications, data, and language models into automated workflows. This two-day programme takes participants from a first trigger to a production-ready AI agent, built around genuine business processes.

Most organisations already hold the components of an automated operation, including web forms, spreadsheets, customer records, and shared inboxes, but connect them manually. n8n unifies these systems on a visual canvas, and its AI nodes allow a language model to read, classify, and act on the information passing between them.

Day one covers workflow fundamentals, including triggers, nodes, data transformation, and integrations. Day two introduces AI capabilities, from document classification and extraction to agents with memory and tools, and concludes with deployment, error handling, and security.

Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, you will be able to

  1. 01

    Construct n8n workflows using triggers, integrations, and data transformation logic.

  2. 02

    Integrate language models into workflows for classification, extraction, and drafting tasks.

  3. 03

    Configure AI agent nodes with memory, tools, and human approval checkpoints.

  4. 04

    Deploy, monitor, and secure workflows for reliable production operation.
