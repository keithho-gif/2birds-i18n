Course title

Professional Ethics in Adult Education

Professional duties, assessment integrity and ethical decision-making

Overview Learning outcomes Programme Who should attend

Overview

Trainers and providers hold a position of trust with learners, employers, and public funds. This workshop examines the ethical obligations that arise in daily practice and how to discharge them with integrity.

Most ethical difficulties in training do not initially present as ethical problems. A learner narrowly short of competency, an employer requesting a favourable outcome, or an apparently harmless referral incentive each carries consequences for learners, for the provider, and for the funding on which it depends.

The session sets out the duties owed by an adult educator, works through the situations in which those duties conflict, and equips participants with a structured method for reaching, recording, and defending a decision. Every module draws on scenarios from Singapore's training sector.

Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, you will be able to

  1. 01

    Articulate the professional duties owed to learners, employers, and the funding authority.

  2. 02

    Manage conflicts of interest arising in assessment, enrolment, and commercial relationships.

  3. 03

    Respond appropriately to plagiarism, impersonation, and AI-generated submissions in accordance with the Rules of Evidence.

  4. 04

    Apply a structured ethical decision framework and maintain a defensible record of the outcome.
