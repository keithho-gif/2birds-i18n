WSQ Curriculum Development

Curriculum developed to industry standards

SWDA approves funding for programmes that satisfy established accreditation standards. 2BIRDS develops the courseware, drafts the course proposal, and prepares all documentation that is required to align your submission directly with the agency guidelines.

I · The funding

SWDA course funding criteria

The two tiers

TierBaselineEnhancedFor
Tier 1 Up to70% Up to90%

The SkillsFuture Career Transition Programme, the SkillsFuture Series, courses that stack towards MOE-funded Full Qualifications offered by the Institutes of Higher Learning, and Workplace Literacy and Numeracy courses.

Tier 2 Up to50% Up to70%

Courses that deliver skills found in the Course Approval Skills List (CASL), WSQ accredited courses supported by the relevant government agencies or appointed entities, and courses recognised under frameworks endorsed by Course Endorsement Bodies (CEBs).

Five stages

Course funding approval process

Each stage in detail
  1. 01

    Stage 01. The basic criteria

    A course must be examinable with clear learning outcomes, meant for employment or job upgrading, answer a skills shortage rather than an oversupply, not be an academic degree, and meet SWDA's Adult Educator qualification requirements. A course that fails any of these is not approved.

    Met
    Continue to 02 →
    Not met
    Not approved
  2. 02

    Stage 02. CASL

    At least half of the skills the course delivers must be found in the Course Approval Skills List. A course that delivers five skills needs three of them on the list.

    Met
    Continue to 04 →
    Not met
    Try the CEB route, 03 →
  3. 03

    Stage 03. CEB

    A course that does not clear the CASL test can still qualify if it is recognised under a regulatory or professional development framework endorsed by a Course Endorsement Body. If it clears neither, it is not approved.

    Recognised
    Continue to 04 →
    Not recognised
    Not approved
  4. 04

    Stage 04. Enrolment cap

    Every Tier 2 funded course carries an enrolment cap. If the cap is fully used before the funding validity period ends, the course must pass quality and relevance checks before funding continues. If the cap is not fully used, the course goes through renewal checks.

    Cap fully used
    Quality and relevance checks, 05 →
    Cap not fully used
    Renewal checks
  5. 05

    Stage 05. Course Quality Checks

    The checks include attendance rates and the share of employer-sponsored trainees. At least 40 per cent of enrolment must be employer-sponsored for the course to stay eligible. A course that fails is given a 30-day grace period to teach out classes already created or begun.

    Passed
    Funding continues
    Failed
    30-day grace period to teach out

What SWDA does not fund

  • Personal enrichment and lifestyle courses.
  • Company-specific courses, such as induction and orientation.
  • Academic degree courses.
  • Seminars and conferences.
  • Overseas courses.
  • Courses with objectionable content.

Source. SWDA, SSG Funding
