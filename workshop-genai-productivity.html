Course title

Generative AI for Professional Practice

Structured prompting, integrated copilots and custom assistants

Overview Learning outcomes Programme Who should attend

Overview

Generative AI assistants are now embedded in email, documents, and spreadsheets, yet most professionals use only a fraction of their capability. This practical workshop establishes the working disciplines that make them dependable everyday tools.

The quality of an AI-assisted result is rarely determined by the tool itself. It depends on how the task is framed, what context is supplied, and how rigorously the output is verified before use.

The session covers structured prompting, the copilots integrated into standard office software, custom assistants for recurring work, and the verification and confidentiality practices that keep AI-assisted work accurate and secure.

Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, you will be able to

  1. 01

    Construct prompts that supply clear context, constraints, and examples.

  2. 02

    Apply integrated copilots across email, documents, spreadsheets, and meetings.

  3. 03

    Configure a custom assistant for a recurring professional task.

  4. 04

    Verify AI-generated output and safeguard confidential information in accordance with organisational policy.
