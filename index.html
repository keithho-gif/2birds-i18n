Important answers

Frequent enquiries.

01How are your advisory fees structured?

Every engagement begins with a scoping discussion to clarify deliverables and project boundaries. We agree on fees through a fixed proposal before work begins, providing predictable costs from the start.

02Can our organisation develop the materials and apply your DACE or DDDLP credentials?

No. We provide our DACE and DDDLP credentials only for curriculum produced within our firm. SWDA guidelines require the named developers to be the actual authors of the courseware, ensuring full compliance during audit reviews.

03What delivery timelines should we expect?

Working as an established team protects projects from the delays often experienced with individual curriculum developers. Target delivery dates are agreed during scoping and specified directly in your proposal.

04What is covered during the initial consultation?

The preliminary consultation examines your curriculum plans and verifies funding eligibility under SWDA frameworks. We provide clear advice on course viability, administrative obligations, and realistic delivery schedules so your team can decide how best to proceed.
